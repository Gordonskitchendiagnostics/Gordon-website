Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Gordon's Kitchen Diagnostics LLC ("Gordon's," "we," "us," or "our") operates Gordon's App, a service-management application used to manage customers, service locations, equipment, service tickets, invoices, payments, and related business records.

Information we collect

We may collect business contact information, customer and service-location information, equipment and service records, invoice and payment-related information, uploaded photos or documents, account information, and technical information needed to operate and secure the application.

QuickBooks information

If you connect Gordon's App to Intuit QuickBooks Online, the application may access and exchange accounting information that you authorize, including customers, invoices, invoice balances, payments, items, and related accounting identifiers. Gordon's App uses this information only to provide the requested QuickBooks synchronization and accounting features.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve Gordon's App, perform service and invoicing workflows, synchronize authorized accounting records, communicate about service activity, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, email delivery, security, and accounting integrations. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect our rights, users, systems, or property.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for business, accounting, legal, security, and operational purposes.

Your choices and disconnecting QuickBooks

You may stop using Gordon's App at any time. A connected QuickBooks company may be disconnected through the application's connection controls or through Intuit's connected-app settings. After disconnection, Gordon's App will no longer request new QuickBooks data unless the connection is authorized again. Records already stored for legitimate accounting or business purposes may be retained as required or permitted by law.
